  • Windows, frames and sills

    The heart of the round. Glass, frames and sills scrubbed and rinsed with pure water every four or eight weeks, upstairs windows included, all from the ground.

  • Conservatory roofs

    Moss, algae and leaf muck lifted off glass and polycarbonate roofs with soft brushes, so the room gets its light back.

  • Gutters and fascias

    Gutters emptied and downpipes checked, so rainwater goes where it should, and the boards along your roofline washed back to their proper colour.

Why we clean with pure water

Tap water carries dissolved minerals, and that’s what leaves spots when it dries. We filter ours down to nothing but water, scrub the glass and frames with a soft brush on a long pole, then rinse. With nothing left in the water to mark the glass, it dries clear on its own.

Questions people ask

Anything else, just ask. A quick call or WhatsApp is usually the fastest way.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can get to the back of the house, an unlocked side gate is fine, we can clean while you’re out. We’ll let you know when we’ve been.

How do you clean upstairs windows without a ladder?

With a water-fed pole. It’s a long, lightweight pole with a soft brush on the end, fed with filtered water. We scrub and rinse from the ground, which is safer for us and means nothing leans against your property.

How often should my windows be cleaned?

Most homes look their best on a four or eight-weekly round. Every four weeks suits busy roads and homes near fields or building work; every eight is plenty for most others. One-off cleans are welcome too.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain doesn’t spoil a pure-water clean. Rain usually leaves marks because it sticks to dirt already on the glass, and we’ve taken that away. We only reschedule for heavy rain, high winds or frost.

Is it safe for plants and pets?

Yes. We don’t use soap or chemicals, only filtered water, so there’s nothing harmful running off into your borders, lawn or pond.

Do you clean conservatory roofs, gutters and fascias on the same visit?

Often, yes. Tick everything you’re interested in on the quote form and we’ll price it together and book it in the way that makes most sense for you.

How do I pay?

We’ll go through payment options when we send your quote, so you can pick whatever’s easiest.
