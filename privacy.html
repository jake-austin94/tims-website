Privacy & cookies

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version. We do not use tracking cookies and we do not sell or share your details with anyone for marketing. If you ask for a quote we use what you tell us to give you that quote and to do the work. That is it.

Who we are

T M Professional Window Cleaning cleans windows, conservatory roofs, gutters and fascias around Newmarket and Cambridge. We are responsible for the information described on this page.

You can reach us at tmprofessionalwindowcleaning@gmail.com or on 07799 963390.

When you ask for a quote

The quote form asks for your name, phone number, postcode, property type and what you would like cleaned. Your email address and a message are optional.

We use those details to work out a price, to contact you about it, and to carry out the work if you go ahead. We need them for exactly that reason: without them we cannot quote. If you give us an email address we send you a short confirmation so you know the request arrived.

We keep quote requests for two years, so that we can look back at what we quoted and pick up where we left off if you come back to us later. If you would rather we deleted yours sooner, just ask.

When you simply visit

We count visits so we know whether the website is doing its job: how many people came, which pages they read, and whether they found us through Google, a link or an advert.

We do this without cookies and without following you around the internet. Each day our server turns your network address and browser into a scrambled code that cannot be turned back into you, and that code changes every night. We never store your network address itself. We cannot tell who you are from any of it, and neither can anyone else.

Alongside that we record which page was viewed, roughly how long for, whether you tapped the call, WhatsApp or email buttons, your rough device type and browser, and the country your connection appears to be in. Visit records are kept for 26 months.

Cookies

This website sets no tracking cookies and no advertising cookies. Because the counting described above uses none, there is nothing here you need to agree to, which is why you are not pestered by a pop-up.

If we ever add something optional, such as measuring an advert, a banner will appear first and nothing optional will load unless you say yes. If you say no, the site works exactly as it does now.

Who else is involved

A few trusted companies handle this information for us, and only on our instructions:

  • GitHub Pages serves the website itself.
  • Supabase stores quote requests and visit counts, on servers in Ireland.
  • n8n passes your quote request from the form to us.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the email we receive and the confirmation you get.

We do not sell your details, and we do not pass them to anyone else for their own marketing.

What you can ask for

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also object to how we are using it. Email tmprofessionalwindowcleaning@gmail.com and we will sort it out, normally within a month and at no cost.

If you are not happy with how we have handled things you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change how any of this works we will update this page and change the date at the top.
