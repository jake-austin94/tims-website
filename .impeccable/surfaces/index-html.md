# Surface: index.html (home, one page)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: local homeowners (older-skewing, mostly on phones) around Newmarket to Cambridge. Action: check their street is covered, then ask for a price (form, call, WhatsApp). Proof: demonstration only (the cleanable window, the pole diagram, the area diagram). No reviews/photos yet; never invent them. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, self-hosted fonts, hero window kept, reduced motion respected.

Memorable moment: cleaning the dirty window yourself, then watching it rinse clear.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The window-cleaning category standard, executed at premium craft: the visitor instantly reads "window cleaner" and trusts the polish. The interactive window is the one thing no competitor has. Refuses the earlier OS-map world (rejected by the owner as too bright and garish) and bargain-trade clip-art.

OWN-WORLD: Deep harbour navy grounds for hero, pure-water and quote; mist-white and white grounds between; one clear window blue for actions and selected states; sky blue for highlights on dark. Manrope throughout, 800 for headings with tight tracking. Soft rounded cards with layered blue-tinted shadows, pill buttons, hairline rules.

STORY: The visitor cleans the window, checks their postcode, picks services from the list, understands pure water, sees four steps, finds their village, asks for a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy hero. Left: headline "Clear windows, kept that way.", lede, postcode check card, white primary "Get a free quote", outline call button, WhatsApp line, village list. Right: the arched sash window with a soft sky glow behind it.

FORM: Category standard (canon), taken by the owner. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
